JANUARY 1 – Wednesday

_MG_7715.JPG

No mail. Hoping to save as much as possible this year. Broke at the moment due to buying Xmas presents and being on 28 days leave. Still have some more presents to buy. Also intend to buy a civy outfit in the coming months. Next pay day Jan. 24th - can I managed without borrowing?

Intend to number all regular correspondance from to-day. Given Z. up as a bad job. No letter for months now. Hoping for big things in the future with G.S.[1]

What does the future year hold for me????

New Year celebrations passed very quietly.

Wrote to Doug. No.1.

JANUARY 2 - Thursday

_MG_7716.JPG

Had letters to-day from Betty, Slater[2] and Doug. Also a Xmas card and chit from Molly.

It rained this evening. The first rain for months.

Answered Betty's letter - No.1.

Answered Doug's letter No.2

Having to stay in billet due to shortage of cash.

Bob leaves to-morrow for Delhi. Been to-gether for over a year now.
Dawn does not write anymore. Wrote to June on 31st Dec asking why?.

JANUARY 3 - Friday

Very warm to-day, warmest for two or three weeks.

Had letter from Dad with £3. A life-saver if ever there was one.

Answered Dad's letter.

Wrote to Mrs Wyke, returning 5/- P.O. unable to change it owing to issuing office stamp being omitted.

Changed a £1 at work and went out to cinema. Went to "Globe" to see F. MacMurray in "SMOKY" not a bad film. Had dinner out. (Chicken, curry and rice).

Bob's posting to Delhi cancelled - wizard show!.

Intend to spend next to nothing in the coming week.

JANUARY 4 – Saturday

_MG_7717.JPG

No mail in to-day.

Had no reply yet to my letter to Pam, written last December.

An unclaimed box at the P.O. yielded a plentiful supply of good books. Plenty of reading in the coming weeks. One way to save money!?

Quite a nice day, seems to be getting over the cold period.

JANUARY 5 – Sunday

Still no mail. Rumours that the camp is closing this month. (wizard show) Our unit and 54 E.U. are said to be moving into Sgts mess, Sgts into Officers mess. This may mean more "bull". But even then the outlook in the way of good food, billets etc; is much better.

I have finished with Colleen[3] for good now, due to her father laying down the law. Might look around some more - might not!? Couldn't care less!!

Went for a walk this evening with Bob and Neville round South Calcutta. What a walk!? Nearly managed to get lost.

JANUARY 6 – Monday

_MG_7718.JPG

No mail for me again to-day. Movements played Cal. Signals to-day, score 1-1. Movements second in league.

Very quiet to-day. Have lent "Char-wallah" 5 Ruffees. Promised to pay back on Friday 10th. Staying in again to-night. I've got to managed without borrowing till 24th. Had M.P's around billet looking for a stolen gramophone. No luck here!

Its about time I had a letter from home. Still plenty of books to read - here goes.

Doc. Stack went on leave to-day to Bangalore.

JANUARY 7 – Tuesday

Still no mail. Four days since the last one. Here's hoping for to-morrow.

More signs of camp closing - having to watch our tables and chairs. Carting such things away.

F/Lt. Pritchard finished work a couple of days ago. Going home in the coming week off[4]. (F/Lt. Harrison now C.O.)

Still no news of my L.A.C. Intend to bind like hell when I get back to work.

Lovely day to-day. Pixi not very well. Can't do much for her.

Wrote to Mother. No.1.

JANUARY 8 – Wednesday

_MG_7719.JPG

Had a letter from Mother.

Very nice day, but nothing doing. Pixie[5] is still unwell. Something will have to be done in a few days if no better. Neville in to-night, this means a night of letter writing. Mother says, the soap I sent has arrived at last. Wants some tea sending for herself and Gordon...

Started moving armoured cars out of the camp. About time too. Half day to-day for the boys – no peace!?

Answered Mother's letter No.2.

JANUARY 9 – Thursday

Received a parcel of writing paper and books from Mrs Wyke. Have written and thanked her to-day, also passed on a bit of gen.

A nice clear day but very cold.

Played football this evenings for first XI against the rest of Movements. Won 4-0. Game a dead loss.

Informed by O. Officer at 7.20 to prepare ourselves for a guard of honour. At 7.30 we were told it was cancelled.

Once more on the rocks with regards to cash. Two more weeks to go before my pay-day. Such is life!!?

JANUARY 10 – Friday

_MG_7720.JPG

Had letters from Betty Slater and Pat Rose.

Not a very nice day – dull and cold. Having to prepare the billet for an inspection by some A.O.C. We have plenty of time to do it in – three days in fact. Had a little "do" with the S.W.O. over tables etc: I came off second best. Nearly put on a charge for being "cheeky".

Feeling under the weather to-day. Don't seem to be able to concentrate on anything in fact. I'm afraid answering the letters will have to wait.

Had to borrow 10 chits from Bob. Maybe I shall be able to pay back before the 24th.
9-15 P.M. I'm going to bed.
That char-wallah paid back the 5 chits I lent him.

JANUARY 11 – Saturday

Had letters from Dirk[6] and Taff Reynolds.

Very cold this morning but warm later on. Had another "do" with S.W.O. threatened to put Neville and Bob on a charge. C. in C. India inspecting billets in the next three days. Load of "bull" for us!?

Answered Pat's letter No.1.
Answered Dirk's letter No.1.
Start work to-morrow.
Answered Betty's letter No.2.
Answered Taff's letter.
10-40 P.M. I'm going to bed.

JANUARY 14 – Tuesday

_MG_7721.JPG

Had a letter from Hockett[7].

Very nice to-day. Not very busy. I'm on lunch-time duty clerk. One plane out to Singapore. Joe has managed to get a table clock for our table also a table lamp. These will be used when we get organised in Sgts mess.

[8]Answered Hockett's letter

Everyone staying in to-night. Another game of indoor cricket to-night. Last night we lost 20 runs to 10 runs! What cricket!

JANUARY 15 – Wednesday (p. 15)

Received Mother's X-mas card to-day. It was posted on [9]5th Dec. Presume it came by sea mail. Apart from this, no mail.

My day-off to-day. Stayed in this morning. This afternoon went to see Movements play RAF Station, Cal. at cricket. Movements won by 64 runs. I was score-keeper – and I didn't "work" the book for our benefit!!?

Wrote to Mother No. 3

F/Lt. Pritchard leaves for Bombay to-morrow. One of the best types I have met. Sorry yet glad he is going home.

JANUARY 16 – Thursday (page 16)

_MG_7722.JPG

Had letters from Molly:- Don and Betty (sister).

Very cold to-day. I wore my blue for the first time in India. Pretty busy at work.

Went out with Taff to see M.K. Taff told her what he thought – and left me to dry the tears. I'm going to see her soon - on my own. Went to see "Two Guys from Milwaukee" a very good film.

M.K. professed her love for Taff - and he is engaged!! A very interesting scene.

Wrote to Betty - No.1

JANUARY 17 – Friday (page 17)

No mail in to-day. Work goes down O.K. very little to-do. Jordie and his girl J. have arranged for me to meet a girl. I hear she is O.K. so I'm hoping. Meeting on Saturday night and I'm making the most of it.

Answered[10] to Don's letter No.1.

Had another game of cricket to-night we won this time by one wicket and ten runs.

Very cold this morning. I have another cold worst luck.

JANUARY 18 – Saturday

_MG_7723.JPG

[struck-through word in a small box][11] Day-off to-day. No mail in, should be plenty to-morrow.

Stayed in the billet during the day. Went out with Jordie down to Julie's to-night. Didn't meet the other two - but had a wizard time. Had a wizard dinner, met a chap from [struck-through word][12] who knew most of the girls I did from that way - including Pat. His name is Ginger Warren. Jordie had his outs as usual. If he gets frosted - I wonder!!!??

Pretty cold all day.

JANUARY 19 – Sunday

Received letters from June Hill, Sheila Holland and Dot Vernon (???). What is behind the letter from Dot? That remains to be found out.

Another cold day to-day. Wore my blue once more. Work going down the same. Start binding to-morrow about that trade-test.

Answered Sheila's letter No.1

Answered June's letter No.1

Wrote to Mother No.4

We haven't heard any more about moving out of this camp. Lets hope its soon.

JANUARY 20—Monday

_MG_7724.JPG

No mail in for me to-day. Most likely some more to-morrow. Trouble is expected in next few days so we have been armed. Went as escort this afternoon to BARRACKPORE. A fairly good run. No trouble encountered. A little warmer to-day. Hope it keeps this up.

Wrote to Dot Vernon

Wrote to Dawn

Hoping for a reply from Dawn. Months since I last heard from Dawn.

Have been detailed for prominent[13] escort duty with 7734[14] — Good show!

JANUARY 21—Tuesday

Still no mail for me.

All troops confined to camp until further notice. Trams went on strike to-day. Trouble is expected, but upto now none has been reported.

A very nice day to-day. Went up to H.Q. this afternoon to watch the match between Move. and 54 E.U. — Move's lost 2-1. A pretty rotten game all round.

Staying in once more to-night. We have a transit Sgt. billeted on us for the next few days. No more news on moving yet. Still no letter from Pam at Bournemouth.

JANUARY 22 – Wednesday

_MG_7725.JPG

Had letters from Doug and also Ruth.

We are still confined to camp. Police opened fire to-day. Forces lorry burnt S. Calcutta. Our own lorry stoned. All quiet around Park St and Chowringhee.

Heard to-day that we are moving to Dum Dum. Nothing definate yet, but it looks like the thing. Plenty to be said for Dum Dum – good and bad.

Didn't do any writing to-night not in the mood. Movements played S.P's at cricket – won by 10 runs.

JANUARY 23 – Thursday

Had a letter from Mum. 30/- the life-saver arrived at last! Pay most of my debts with this.

All quiet to-day. We are once more allowed out. The move to Dum Dum is definate. Move in two or three weeks time. Work going down O.K.

Answered Mum's letter No 5.

Neville now having to stay in billet every night. M.B. gone to England.

Answered Doug's letter No 3.

JANUARY 24 – Friday

_MG_7726.JPG

No mail in to-day. My first pay-day this year. Every bit of it has gone on debts. Still got the 30/-. I am not going to borrow till next pay-day. By the [?][15] I should be at Dum Dum. In a way I'm looking forward to the move. It will be a change. Very warm to-day. My day off, but I have stayed in as I mean to stay in for the next two weeks. I must not borrow again.

I'm cheesed off.

Parade in the morning.

JANUARY 25 – Saturday

Parade was an absolute farce this morning. 54 E.U. didn't have a clue!! This will most likely be my last parade with Movements, should be at Dum Dum. F/Lt Harrison is thinking of leaving Bob and Neville at Victoria House. I'm going to be on my own up there if that's the case. No more news yet about our L.A.C. apart from that letter Osborne received.

Answered Ruth's letter

No mail in for me to-day. Had dinner with Rena[16] to-night. Quite an interesting evening.

JANUARY 26 - Sunday

_MG_7727.JPG

Had this morning off to play football, but it didn't come off. Went to work this afternoon and also did duty clerk with Mev.[17] Didn't do much work - played cards all the time.

Plenty of mail in to-day - but none for me. It's about time I was having some. Haven't had a letter from Pam yet. No joy from that quarter.

All the boys are staying in to-night - what a change. They are having a friendly talk at the moment.

Wrote to Mother No.6

JANUARY 27 - Monday

Had letters from Mum, Dirk and Pam.

My day-off, but I spent it in the billet. In bed till dinner (tiffin) time. In Dirk's letter was a photo of Maureen. Must write to her. Pam has now left school and is away from home. Must get a photo. Bob B., Neville and Len on a charge to-morrow for a dirty bed-space. Poor show!! Moving to Dum Dum next Monday at 4 a.m.

Answered Mum's letter No.7

Answered Dirk's letter No.2

Answered Pam's letter No.1

23-00 hrs. - Bed!!!

JANUARY 28—Tuesday

_MG_7728.JPG

No mail in to-day. Hoping for some to-morrow. Nothing new at work to-day or on our move to Dum Dum. Plenty of work to-morrow, taken over bills again. Neville, Bob B. and Len went before C.O. - case amolished. If Bob B. gets one more charge he is sure to loose his tapes. Lucky not here to-day[18] we have to make our own beds. S.W.O has been round shooting the dogs. Penny has been shot. He stood and watched it die. Everyone is staying in again to-night. Whats wrong?!?!! Have to go for all my "jabs" in the next day or two. I shall have to have them.

JANUARY 29—Wednesday

No mail for me to-day. The S.W.O shot Pixi to-day. No dogs left now. Someone (?) put a dog on his desk last night! What a laugh! on the S.W.O of course. Its a dead loss on this camp; be glad when I move, which will be about the 5th of next month.

Nothing new at work to-day, plenty to do. Dr. H.[19] wants me to go down on Sunday. Her hubby will be there this time.

Wrote to Maureen Short.

Wonder how she will take it ??

JANUARY 30 – Thursday

_MG_7729.JPG

No mail in to-day. My day-off. Went out in the after-noon with Bob to pictures and for a good meal. The picture was very good. Its the first time I have been out for weeks.

Moving to Dun Dum on Sunday. Nevill and Bob to work at Victoria House. I will be able to go out with one or the other on my days-off.

S.W.O shot another dog to-day. Only one or two left in the camp now.

— .. —

JANUARY 31 – Friday

Once more no mail for me. Dead loss.

Started moving at the office to-day. What a mess. Be glad when its all over. I'm on day work, no shifts - wizard show.

Once more I am broke. Pay-day next Friday - what a life. I mean to save at D.D.

Wrote to Mother No. 8.

Cpl Jeens went home to-night. New from in-to-night[20], not a bad one. Weather getting warmer. No news of my LAC. yet. Going to see the Adj. in the morning.

One more month gone!

FEBRUARY 1 – Saturday (p. 32)

_MG_7730.JPG

Had letters from Betty Slater and Mrs. Wyke. – 5/- P.O. returned – stamped. Borrowed 10 chits from F/O. Woodford. Shall pay back on Friday. Still moving the desks. Our kit and ourselves go to-morrow. Not a bad day – it's hellish cold to-night.

[struck-through word][21] Answered Betty's letter No.3

Been cleared off to-day. Have passengers in that that. I'm taking the tables and shelf with me to D.D. I shall not be able to see Mena to-morrow. Couldn't care less.

FEBRUARY 2 – Sunday (p. 33)

No mail in for me to-day. Arrived at Dum Dum this afternoon after escorting office desks etc. up here. Our office looks right onto the air-strip and apron. Planes coming and going all day long. We are now in closer contact with P.&F. etc.

The billets are good. We are living with Flying Control at the moment. A pleasant crowd of chaps. The new bearer is only a chikko, but he seems O.K.

I'm dead tired and I'm going to bed – 1930 hrs!

FEBRUARY 3 – Monday (p. 34)

_MG_7731.JPG

Once more no mail for me. It's a dead loss this mail business.

Work going down well. Plenty of planes coming in. This new diversion is causing quite a stir amongst the staff. We are out in the country now and I'm lapping up the fresh air. My appetite is nearly twice as big already. A jungle on the other side of the strip begs investigating; must do so on my day-off.

Wrote to Mrs. Wyke

FEBRUARY 4 – Tuesday (p. 35)

Still no mail.

Arrangements have been made to [illegible, crossed out and overwritten][22] mail [illegible] balance [illegible] to our new office. [illegible]

Work going down O.K. Plenty to do and I've been a bit [cheesed?] [illegible] dashing here and [there?][23].

We have a parade on Friday. Wonder how it will go down? We are not[24] looking forward to it. Such is life.

WROTE MOTHER. No.9.

Going to pictures to see "The Killers" – only 8 annas.

FEBRUARY 5—Wednesday

_MG_7732.JPG

Still no mail. Its about time I had some.

Not much work to do. Forgot to make passenger tickets etc. out this afternoon.

I havent a cent to my name, but I'm going on O.K. any position the worst[25].

P[ink blot][26] Plenty of entertainment there - billiards and tennis - Just the gen!

Havent had a reply to my letter to Dawn yet.

FEBRUARY 6—Thursday

Had letters from Mum and Pat Rose.

Having a day off to-day, its about time I had one. The parade in the morning is also the pay parade - the ^bad[27] part of it. There seems to be bags of "bull" attached to it. W/Cdr Woods was going to have a billet inspection this morning - but it didnt come off. There is one to-morrow.

Its quite warm, in fact hot at Dum Dum compared to Calcutta.

Answered Pat's letter No.2.

FEBRUARY 7 – Friday

_MG_7733.JPG

Pay-day – 50 Ruppees. No mail in for me to-day. C.O's parade this morning, O.K. at best[28], not as much "bull" as I expected. Had to do domestic duties afterwards. Wiping the windows in the canteen against loose-wallahs. Quite a nice change from the routine work. Went to the office this afternoon but didn't do very much.

Quite a warm – in fact a hot day to-day. Need a shower every day now. Went to the pictures to-night to see "White Tie and Tails", a very good film.

Answered Mum's letter No.10

FEBRUARY 8 – Saturday

Had a letter from Don.

A very hot day to-day. Nothing new at work. Taffy took me round the Netherlands Great "Skymaster" to-day. Wizard inside. Everything one could wish for (nearly) is there.

Day off to-morrow. Going in the jungle with Paddy. Take some snaps etc.

Played football this evening against P. & F. – lost 2-0. A very good game – the best I've had in India. Went to see Abbot and Costello in "Time of our lives". A wizard picture.

Answered Don's letter No.2.

FEBRUARY 9 – Sunday

_MG_7734.JPG

No mail in to-day. A very nice day. Not as warm as usual. Went over in the jungle in the morning. Quite good. Sent a couple of wog kids up the palms for cocoanuts. The jungle is quite dense and stretches for miles.

An RAF Lancastrian in to-day bound for Cairo. Managed to take a snap of it.

Haven't been down into Calcutta yet – find plenty to do up here.

AOC inspection and parade to-morrow – lots of bull!

FEBRUARY 10 – Monday (page 41)

Had a letter from Betty (sister) to-day. My letter of the 16th Jan. doesn't seem to have arrived home. My Xmas parcel has arrived home. No news yet of Betty Slater's getting home.

A.O.C. inspection is to-morrow, and I'm not on it! Wizard!! Not much work in to-day. Went to the pictures to-night to see "Anchors Aweigh" – very good film.

Answered Betty's letter No.2

No letter from Dawn yet; nor from Birmingham.

FEBRUARY 11—Tuesday

_MG_7735.JPG

No mail in to-day. The A.O.C. had a visit to the office, but he didn't ask any questions.

It's getting very hot now. Time for more sun-bathing. Quite a good bit of work to-do.

Went to pictures to night.

FEBRUARY 12—Wednesday

Still no mail in. Two 10F's are somewhere on the way here with mail.

A very warm day to-day. My day off so I did a bit of sun-bathing. Once more went to the pictures. Must cut down on the films. On the chow wallah too. I'm spending too much on them.

Not much doing to-day

44 – FEBRUARY 13 – Thursday

_MG_7736.JPG

Had letters from Mum: Molly: A. Lockett: Dot Vernon: June: S. Holland: What a file! Making up for lost time.

Plenty of work to-do to-day.

I hear from G/c. Brighton-Biggie[29] that our office (ABC) will most likely close in another two months. If it does I'm applying for a posting. A very warm day.

Answered Mum's letter No.11.

Answered Molly's letter No.2.

Answered June's letter No.2.

45 – FEBRUARY 14 – Friday

No mail in to-day. The 10F is delayed 48 hours. Had a working parade is morning. It's just a waste of time, didn't arrive at work until 9-30.

Nothing much doing at work. Made one or two books, but nothing to worry about.

Answered Dot Vernon's letter.

For the first time Paddy and I won all three games at snooker.

FEBRUARY 15—Saturday

_MG_7737.JPG

Still no mail in. My day off to-day.

Not feeling too well to-day. It's pretty hot and I've done a spot of sunbathing.

Once more I'm broke, with one more week to go. I have bought everything I require so I shouldn't need to borrow, and I don't intend too.

Answered Sheila's letter No.2.

It has rained a little this evening.

FEBRUARY 16—Sunday

Had quite a big storm early this morning. Most unusual. It's been drizzling till tiffin time, first rain for weeks. Loads of females in the waiting room[30] to-day. I didn't have very much time to relax[31]. I've been in on my own to-day and I've had plenty to do.

Still no mail in. The York is due in to-night, I hope it arrives.

Answered H. Lockett's letter

I'm feeling a little cheesed so I'm going to bed.

FEBRUARY 17—Monday

_MG_7738.JPG

No mail in for me to-day. There is one at Victoria House, a thick one so Joe says – wonder what it can be? Shall know in the morning anyway.

Quite hot to-day and I'm getting that "sticky" feeling once more. Plenty of work to do. Once more it all came at tiffin time, a couple of groupies[32] etc. cannot wait – worst luck. Julia rang up to-day and we are of the opinion that she has joined the "Puddin' Club"!?

Wrote to Mum No. 12.

FEBRUARY 18—Tuesday

No mail arrived in this morning. Had the letter from Victoria House. It was from Brenda Scaife. She is in hospital having the old birth mark removed. Hope it proves successful.

Reported sick this morning with my ears. Nearly deaf in the right but no wax to be found – ? I wonder??

A very cold and dull day to-day. I'm feeling very cold indeed. York arrived in this evening. I hope there is mail for me to-morrow.

Answered Brenda's letter

WROTE TO ZOE!!? – I wonder why I did that? and will there be a reply????

50 — FEBRUARY 19 — Wednesday

_MG_7739.JPG

Had letters from Betty, Dawn, Betty (sis) and Dirk.

Pretty cold to-day but a bit better than before. Nothing much doing at work. Borrowed 11/8 [?][33] from P/O Johnson. Pay back on Friday. Most likely go into Calcutta this weekend to have my photo taken. No letter yet from Maureen, I wonder if she will write?

Answered Betty's (sis) letter No.3
Answered Dirk's letter No.3.
Answered Dawn's letter No.2.

FEBRUARY 20 — Thursday

Had a letter from Maureen Short. Didn't ever expect a reply to mine.

Nice and warm to-day. Seems to be bucking up at last. Nothing much doing at work. No news of my L.A.C. yet. Hear Wyatt is getting his temp. Cpl. !!! Only been in the R.A.F. 12 months. Decided to go slow [?][34] until I get some action on my trade test. York aircraft came in to-night — wonder if there will be any mail on it for me???

Answered Betty Slater's letter No.4.
Answered Maureen's letter.

Parade in morning — must have a shave — now!! and then to bed.

FEBRUARY 21 – Friday

_MG_7740.JPG

Pay-day – 50. Ruppees.

No mail in to-day. A very nice day.

Working parade this morning went wrong!! F/O. Johnson didn't turn up, A.B.C's names were not on the roll – so we spent the morning in the canteen.

Decided to go into Cal to-morrow and have my photo taken.

Didn't do any work to-day, wandering around S.H.Q. getting my pass ready for to-morrow.

FEBRUARY 22 – Saturday

No mail in once more.

Went by bus into Calcutta, what a ride! Called in to see Joe, then I went to see "Anna and the King of Siam" at the Lighthouse. A very good film. Had a wizard meal in the American Kitchen. Then went on prowl for a woman – and got one (5 Rps) for the first time in my life!! Not a bad feeling. Hope to God I'm O.K.!!? Check with M.O. on Monday.

Had my ears cleaned out this morning – they are O.K. now, but have to check with M.O. on Monday to see if infection has set in.

FEBRUARY 23 – Sunday

_MG_7741.JPG

Still no mail in.

A scorching day to-day. I have been playing cricket most of the day. Picked up plenty of sun-burn – not to bad, I don't think I will peel. I'm dead tired and I'm going to bed early.

FEBRUARY 24 – Monday

Still no mail in to-day. A very nice day to-day. On Wednesday Jock and I are going to the Victoria Swimming Pool for the day. Hope its a good day.

Nothing much doing at work – and I'm doing even less! Till some action is taken on that trade-test I took in August. Will I ever get my LAC on that trade-test.

FEBRUARY 25 — Tuesday

_MG_7742.JPG

Still no mail in for me from U.K.

A very warm day, hope it keeps it up for to-morrow.

A York aircraft arrived in from the U.K. this evening. Its about time, two planes have been cancelled. Hope there is some mail in for me.

Cash isn't lasting out as good as I hoped, but things are better and I hope the next pay-day will see me put some by.

Wrote to Mother No 13.

FEBRUARY 26 — Wednesday

Had letters from Mother: Betty (Sis) and Pat Rose. Its about time I had some.

Went with Jock to the Victoria swimming pool to-day. Had a wizard time. It was a glorious day.

When we arrived back we went to the camp cinema to see "Our Vine grows tender Grapes" A very good picture indeed and I really enjoyed it.

I'll try to write to the people at home, start to-morrow.

FEBRUARY 27 – Thursday

Method and process-level changes

All four volumes (_MG_7715 to _MG_7898) were read first to build a master index of people, ranks, units and places. A word was corrected only when a transcription note flagged it as uncertain and the same entity is attested elsewhere in the year with no risk of two separate people (the double-entity exclusion). Where that standard was not met, the text stays as written and the note goes to the Appendix. The diarist’s own spellings (“definate”, “gorgious”, “Paulin’s”, “N°” and similar), slang and offensive period terms are untouched. The following apply to every page and are not repeated in the table below:

  • Transcriber note blocks were removed from the page text (reproduced verbatim under “Original notes”), as were tool headings such as “Transcription”.
  • Page-number remarks printed as separate lines (listed below) were moved out of the running text.
  • Markdown separator lines (---) were dropped; markdown strikethrough, underline (__x__, <u>) and italic markers were rendered as the formatting they describe.
  • Transcriber’s bracketed reconstructions of cropped letters, “[sic]”, “[?]” and “[illegible]” placeholders are kept as supplied.
