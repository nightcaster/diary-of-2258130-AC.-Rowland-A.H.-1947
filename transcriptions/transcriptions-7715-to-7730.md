**JANUARY 1 – Wednesday**

No mail. Hoping to save as much as possible this year. Broke at the moment due to buying Xmas presents and being on 28 days leave. Still have some more presents to buy. Also intend to buy a civy outfit in the coming months. Next pay day Jan. 24th - can I managed without borrowing?

Intend to number all regular correspondance from to-day. Given Z. up as a bad job. No letter for months now. Hoping for big things in the future with G.S.

What does the future year hold for me????

New Year celebrations passed very quietly.

Wrote to Doug. No.1.

---

- *The spellings "managed" (after "can I") and "correspondance" are kept as written.*
- *"Z." is an initial, kept as it appears.*
- *The initials after "with" are uncertain. They could be "G.S." or "J.S.".*
- *The last line is underlined in the original.*
- *The "1" at the foot of the page is the printed page number.*

**JANUARY 2 - Thursday**

Had letters to-day from Betty, Slater and Doug. Also a Xmas card and chit from Molly.

It rained this evening. The first rain for months.

Answered Betty's letter - No.1.

Answered Doug's letter No.2

Having to stay in billet due to shortage of cash.

Bob leaves to-morrow for Delhi. Been to-gether for over a year now.
Dawn does not write anymore. Wrote to June on 31st Dec asking why?.

**JANUARY 3 - Friday**

Very warm to-day, warmest for two or three weeks.

Had letter from Dad with £3. A life-saver if ever there was one.

Answered Dad's letter.

Wrote to Mrs Wyke, returning 5/- P.O. unable to change it owing to issuing office stamp being omitted.

Changed a £1 at work and went out to cinema. Went to "Globe" to see F. MacMurray in "SMOKY" not a bad film. Had dinner out. (Chicken, curry and rice).

Bob's posting to Delhi cancelled - wizard show!.

Intend to spend next to nothing in the coming week.

---

**JANUARY 4 – Saturday**

No mail in to-day.

Had no reply yet to my letter to Pam, written last December.

An unclaimed box at the P.O. yielded a plentiful supply of good books. Plenty of reading in the coming weeks. One way to save money!?

Quite a nice day, seems to be getting over the cold period.

**JANUARY 5 – Sunday**

Still no mail. Rumours that the camp is closing this month. (wizard show) Our unit and 54 E.U. are said to be moving into Sgts mess, Sgts into Officers mess. This may mean more "bull". But even then the outlook in the way of good food, billets etc; is much better.

I have finished with Colleen for good now, due to her father laying down the law. Might look around some more - might not!? Couldn't care less!!

Went for a walk this evening with Bob and Neville round South Calcutta. What a walk!? Nearly managed to get lost.

**Transcription notes:**

- The name "Colleen" has an ambiguous opening letter, and "Calcutta" is slightly compressed in the ink.

**JANUARY 6 – Monday**

No mail for me again to-day. Movements played Cal. Signals to-day, score 1-1. Movements second in league.

Very quiet to-day. Have lent "Char-wallah" 5 Ruffees. Promised to pay back on Friday 10th. Staying in again to-night. I've got to managed without borrowing till 24th. Had M.P's around billet looking for a stolen gramophone. No luck here!

Its about time I had a letter from home. Still plenty of books to read - here goes.

Doc. Stack went on leave to-day to Bangalore.

**JANUARY 7 – Tuesday**

Still no mail. Four days since the last one. Here's hoping for to-morrow.

More signs of camp closing - having to watch our tables and chairs. Carting such things away.

F/Lt. Pritchard finished work a couple of days ago. Going home in the coming week off. (F/Lt. Harrison now C.O.)

Still no news of my L.A.C. Intend to bind like hell when I get back to work.

Lovely day to-day. Pixi not very well. Can't do much for her.

Wrote to Mother. No.1.

Had a letter from Mother.

Very nice day, but nothing doing. Pixie is still unwell. Something will have to be done in a few days if no better. Neville in to-night, this means a night of letter writing. Mother says, the soap I sent has arrived at last. Wants some tea sending for herself and Gordon...

**JANUARY 10 – Friday**

Had letters from Betty Slater and Pat Rose.

Not a very nice day – dull and cold. Having to prepare the billet for an inspection by some A.O.C. We have plenty of time to do it in – three days in fact. Had a little "do" with the S.W.O. over tables etc: I came off second best. Nearly put on a charge for being "cheeky".

Feeling under the weather to-day. Don't seem to be able to concentrate on anything in fact. I'm afraid answering the letters will have to wait.

Had to borrow 10 chits from Bob. Maybe I shall be able to pay back before the 24th.
9-15 P.M. I'm going to bed.

Answered Dirk's letter No.1.

Answered Betty's letter No.2.
Answered Taff's letter.
10-40 P.M. I'm going to bed.

JANUARY 14 – Tuesday

Had a letter from Hockett.

Very nice to-day. Not very busy. I'm on lunch-time duty clerk. One plane out to Singapore. Joe has managed to get a table clock for our table also a table lamp. These will be used when we get organised in Sgts mess.

Answered Hockett's letter

Everyone staying in to-night. Another game of indoor cricket to-night. Last night we lost 20 runs to 10 runs! What cricket!

JANUARY 15 – Wednesday (p. 15)

Received Mother's X-mas card to-day. It was posted on 5th Dec. Presume it came by sea mail. Apart from this, no mail.

My day-off to-day. Stayed in this morning. This afternoon went to see Movements play RAF Station, Cal. at cricket. Movements won by 64 runs. I was score-keeper – and I didn't "work" the book for our benefit!!?

Wrote to Mother No. 3

F/Lt. Pritchard leaves for Bombay to-morrow. One of the best types I have met. Sorry yet glad he is going home.

JANUARY 20—Monday

No mail in for me to-day. Most likely some more to-morrow. Trouble is expected in next few days so we have been armed. Went as escort this afternoon to BARRACKPORE. A fairly good run. No trouble encountered. A little warmer to-day. Hope it keeps this up.

Wrote to Dot Vernon

Wrote to Dawn

Hoping for a reply from Dawn. Months since I last heard from Dawn.

Have been detailed for prominent escort duty with 7734 — Good show!

JANUARY 21—Tuesday

Still no mail for me.

All troops confined to camp until further notice. Trams went on strike to-day. Trouble is expected, but upto now none has been reported.

A very nice day to-day. Went up to H.Q. this afternoon to watch the match between Move. and 54 E.U. — Move's lost 2-1. A pretty rotten game all round.

Staying in once more to-night. We have a transit Sgt. billeted on us for the next few days. No more news on moving yet. Still no letter from Pam at Bournemouth.

**JANUARY 22 – Wednesday**

Had letters from Doug and also Ruth.

We are still confined to camp. Police opened fire to-day. Forces lorry burnt S. Calcutta. Our own lorry stoned. All quiet around Park St and Chowringhee.

Heard to-day that we are moving to Dum Dum. Nothing definate yet, but it looks like the thing. Plenty to be said for Dum Dum – good and bad.

Didn't do any writing to-night not in the mood. Movements played S.P's at cricket – won by 10 runs.

**JANUARY 23 – Thursday**

Had a letter from Mum. 30/- the life-saver arrived at last! Pay most of my debts with this.

All quiet to-day. We are once more allowed out. The move to Dum Dum is definate. Move in two or three weeks time. Work going down O.K.

Answered Mum's letter No 5.

Neville now having to stay in billet every night. M.B. gone to England.

Answered Doug's letter No 3.

---

**JANUARY 24 – Friday**

No mail in to-day. My first pay-day this year. Every bit of it has gone on debts. Still got the 30/-. I am not going to borrow till next pay-day. By the [?] I should be at Dum Dum. In a way I'm looking forward to the move. It will be a change. Very warm to-day. My day off, but I have stayed in as I mean to stay in for the next two weeks. I must not borrow again.

I'm cheesed off.

Parade in the morning.

**JANUARY 25 – Saturday**

Parade was an absolute farce this morning. 5/4 E.U. didn't have a clue!! This will most likely be my last parade with Movements, should be at Dum Dum. F/Lt Harrison is thinking of leaving Bob and Neville at Victoria House. I'm going to be on my own up there if that's the case. No more news yet about our L.A.C. apart from that letter Osborne received.

__Answered Ruth's letter__

No mail in for me to-day. Had dinner with Rena to-night. Quite an interesting evening.

JANUARY 28—Tuesday

No mail in to-day. Hoping for some to-morrow. Nothing new at work to-day or on our move to Dum Dum. Plenty of work to-morrow, taken over bills again. Neville, Bob B. and Len went before C.O. - case amolished. If Bob B. gets one more charge he is sure to loose his tapes. Lucky not here to-day we have to make our own beds. S.W.O has been round shooting the dogs. Penny has been shot. He stood and watched it die. Everyone is staying in again to-night. Whats wrong?!?!! Have to go for all my "jabs" in the next day or two. I shall have to have them.

JANUARY 29—Wednesday

No mail for me to-day. The S.W.O shot Pixi to-day. No dogs left now. Someone (?) put a dog on his desk last night! What a laugh! on the S.W.O of course. Its a dead loss on this camp; be glad when I move, which will be about the 5th of next month.

Nothing new at work to-day, plenty to do. Dr. H. wants me to go down on Sunday. Her hubby will be there this time.

Wrote to Maureen Short.

Wonder how she will take it ??

Had letters from Betty Slater and Mrs. Wyke. – 5/- P.O. returned – stamped. Borrowed 10 chits from F/O. Woodford. Shall pay back on Friday. Still moving the desks. Our kit and ourselves go to-morrow. Not a bad day – it's hellish cold to-night.

~~[struck-through word]~~ Answered Betty's letter No.3

Been cleared off to-day. Have passengers in ~~that~~ that. I'm taking the tables and shelf with me to D.D. I shall not be able to see Mena to-morrow. Couldn't care less.

No mail in for me to-day. Arrived at Dum Dum this afternoon after escorting office desks etc. up here. Our office looks right onto the air-strip and apron. Planes coming and going all day long. We are now in closer contact with P.&F. etc.

The billets are good. We are living with Flying Control at the moment. A pleasant crowd of chaps. The new bearer is only a chikko, but he seems O.K.

I'm dead tired and I'm going to bed – 1930 hrs!
